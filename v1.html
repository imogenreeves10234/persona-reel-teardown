What worked — the hard parts. Fifteen seconds in one unbroken take with essentially no drift (0.02 change across the whole clip), identity held throughout, and the golden-hour rim light landed. Wind carries the motion so the body barely has to perform — that is what makes an uninterrupted 15 s viable at all.
What failed, and why it's the prompt's fault. The prompt asked for a medium shot "filling most of the frame width" and then described "the land falls away to open ocean… a cove and hills far off… depth stretching to the horizon." Two instructions answering the same question, and the vista won — the camera pulled back to show the view it was told about. Fix: write the background as compressed and behind her, never as a view worth looking at, and state framing as how much of the frame she occupies.
