Why a crop and not a better prompt. Four paired tests showed the prompt has almost no authority over framing on this model: word-ratio refuted, camera-block negations refuted (0.544 vs 0.522), flat backgrounds worth only +0.05, and asking for a dramatically tighter crop worth +0.034 against a 0.17 gap. The prompt's own spread across four seeds was 0.622–0.756. Geometry closes exactly what language could not, for free, with no upscaling.
